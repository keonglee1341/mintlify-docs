Welcome aboard. This page walks you from your first day to your first shipped contribution. Skim the whole thing once, then work through the steps.

## Before you begin

You'll need:

- A welcome message from your team lead confirming your role
- Access to the team's communication channel and shared drive
- An account for the tools your role uses

## Your first week

<Steps>
  <Step title="Meet the team">
    Introduce yourself in the team channel. Note who owns which area — your lead will point you to the right people.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your access">
    Join the shared drive, accept your repository invite, and turn on notifications for the channels that matter to your role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the playbook">
    Skim this site end to end. You don't need to memorize it — just know what exists and where to look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take a small task">
    Pick up a low-risk task with a clear definition of done. Ask questions early; nobody expects you to guess.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ship and review">
    Walk your work through review with your lead. That review is the real onboarding — it shows you how we build.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How a build runs

| Stage | What happens | Who leads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan | Scope agreed, owner named, done defined | Team lead |
| Build | Small reviewable steps, team kept in the loop | Builder |
| Raise | Testing, docs, handover | Builder and reviewer |
| Crown | Launch, retro, learnings captured | Whole team |

## Ways of working

- **Small steps.** Break work into pieces someone else can review in one sitting.
- **Say the status.** Post where things stand before you go quiet, not after.
- **Ask early.** A five-minute question beats a day of guessing.
- **Leave it better.** Update the page or note that confused you.

<Tip>
  Stuck on something? Ask in the team channel first — someone has almost certainly hit it before.
</Tip>
