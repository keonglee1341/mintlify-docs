## What we build

We take an idea from empty ground to a finished build:

- **Plan** — shape the scope, agree what "done" means, and name an owner.
- **Build** — work in small, reviewable steps with the team in the loop.
- **Raise** — test, document, and hand over something people can actually use.
- **Crown** — celebrate the launch, capture what we learned, and carry it forward.
